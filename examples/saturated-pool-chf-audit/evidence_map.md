## Search strategy and date

On 2026-08-02, the audit checked the public OSTI identity for Zuber's report and the NIST WebBook property interface. A complete novelty or validation search was not performed because no new mechanism is claimed.

## Inclusion and exclusion criteria

Include sources that expose the exact hydrodynamic derivation, coefficient convention, saturation property state, or independent pool-boiling benchmark. Exclude flow-boiling, dryout, or confined-channel data from direct validation of this pool-boiling baseline.

## Competing or contradictory evidence

Macrolayer depletion, dry-spot connectivity, contact-line/recoil, liquid-supply, stochastic, and conjugate-heater mechanisms may match terminal CHF while predicting different intermediate observables.

## Novelty threats

The model is classical and no novelty is claimed. A new coefficient, rearrangement, or fitted surface factor is not a new causal mechanism by itself.
